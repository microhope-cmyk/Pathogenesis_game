updates

A running, plain-language record of what's changed in the game — newest first.

September 24, 2026

Pathogens get sneakier, plus a faster way to play

Several pathogen cards now act more like their real-world counterparts: some heal themselves back up over time, some release toxins each turn they stick around, one can go dormant and hide before striking later, one strikes back if you attack it and don't finish it off, and a few spread copies of themselves the longer they're left alone. There's also a new "Short Game" option when starting a match — energy builds up faster and starting health is lower, so a full game can wrap up in just a few minutes. The in-game tutorial now explains Health — how you actually win or lose — right up front, and a couple of small mistakes in the game's own instructions were cleaned up.

September 21, 2026

A new logo

Pathogenesis has a new logo, now showing on the sign-in screen and in the corner of the game itself.

September 10, 2026

A big balance pass

Several cards were reworked to feel fairer and more interesting to play, a handful of new ones were added, and the turn-by-turn history now spells out exactly what happened in every fight — who hit whom, and for how much. Dice rolls for certain long-term complications now flash on screen so you can see exactly what was rolled, not just the outcome.

September 9, 2026

Online play, fixed

Two-player games where each person is on their own device were badly broken — a move made by one player often never reached the other. That's fixed now, along with a separate bug that could freeze the game during the computer's turn in solo mode.

September 8, 2026

More feedback on what's happening

The game now shows a flash and a floating number whenever either side's Health takes a hit, and pathogen cards animate differently depending on how they were destroyed. Cards were also split into a core set you'll see in every game and a smaller bonus set held back for later.

September 7, 2026 — Launch

Pathogenesis goes live

Pathogenesis is a card game about the immune system fighting off infection — play the body's defenses against invading pathogens in quick head-to-head matches, either against a friend or against the computer. This first version includes a guided tutorial for learning the rules, a solo mode against the computer, and simple sign-in so a guest can jump straight into a game with no account needed.
